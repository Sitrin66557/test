01 / About

The Man Behind the Lens

Hey, I'm Dave — a photographer born and raised in Boise, Idaho. For over 12 years I've been chasing light across the Gem State, from the mirror-flat Snake River at dawn to the wildflower meadows of the Sawtooth Wilderness.

Photography, to me, is about connection. Whether I'm photographing a couple on their wedding day, a family's annual portrait, or the solitary grandeur of a mountain ridge at golden hour — I believe every frame tells a story worth preserving.

My work has been featured in Idaho Magazine, Outdoor Photographer, and the Boise Weekly. When I'm not behind the lens, you'll find me fly fishing the Henry's Fork or hiking trails with my dog, Scout.
